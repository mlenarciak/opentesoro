# Authorized Agicap account observations

Observed on 2026-11-12 through an authenticated browser session authorized by the account owner. Navigation was read-only. No business data was created, modified, deleted, exported into this repository, or used in fixtures. Credentials, cookies, tokens and private responses are not part of OpenTesoro.

The account reported **no active subscription**, **trial/synthetic data**, and **read-only treasury access**. It warned that synchronization had stopped and some features were unavailable. These restrictions limit what can be concluded about the complete commercial product.

Observed Italian navigation:

- Tesoreria (cash-flow forecast).
- Banca (bank transactions, all-transactions view).
- In attesa (expected transactions) with certainty, overdue, frequency and suggested-frequency views.
- Prima nota (bank journal).
- Area Finanziaria (financing/debt).
- Dashboards (reporting).

The interface distinguishes actual banking activity from expected cash flows, supports multiple reporting views and exposes contextual access restrictions. OpenTesoro models these domains independently rather than depending on the private application API. No claim of complete paid-product behavior or feature parity is based on this account inspection.

For current supported integration contracts, use [current-api.md](current-api.md). The standalone OpenTesoro dataset is entirely synthetic and independent of this account.
